# Daily lesson — 2026-11-18

Wednesday is **anime and stories day**. Video 1 is an anime quiz: five multiple-choice questions about **ドラえもん** (Doraemon), the robot cat everyone in Japan knows. Video 2 is a true story from the same teacher about her daughter, who missed her favorite nursery-school teacher so much that she cried at night, and what happened when they went back to visit.

Two new conversation tools today. **〜たことがありますか？** asks "have you ever ~?", one of the first things a Japanese person will ask you about Japan (ラーメンを食べたことがありますか？). And **「…」と言いました** lets you report what someone said, which is how you tell a story.

**Time:** about 19 minutes of video, plus exercises, a reading piece on a sweets-shop menu, and two phrases to say out loud.

**How to watch:** Play each video once all the way through without pausing to look things up. Both are labeled Beginner, one notch above most of last week's videos. That's on purpose. The quiz repeats every answer choice two or three times, and the story uses lots of pictures. Japanese subtitles are fine; skip English if you can.

Listen for words you already know coming back: 好き/大好き, 猫, 部屋, 寝ています, 住んでいます, 先生, 娘, 写真, 〜ヶ月, 〜に見えます, 〜てみます, 〜たいです, 〜ました/〜ませんでした, 〜ています.

## Video 1: Doraemon quiz (9:56)

[Doraemon Quiz | Beginner Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mNFeCQ5VsGM) — にほんごのじかん | Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)

She introduces Doraemon and Nobita, then asks five three-choice questions (３択クイズ): his favorite food, what animal he's a robot of, what he's scared of, where he sleeps, and what century he came from. After each answer she explains why. Play along: say your answer out loud before she reveals it.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| キャラクター | kyarakutā | character |
| アニメ | anime | anime |
| えいが / 映画 | eiga | movie |
| まんが / 漫画 | manga | manga, comics |
| 〜たことがありますか | ~ta koto ga arimasu ka | have you ever ~? |
| だい〜もん / 第〜問 | dai ~mon | question number ~ |
| せいかい / 正解 | seikai | correct answer |
| どらやき / どら焼き | dorayaki | pancake sandwich with sweet bean filling |
| わがし / 和菓子 | wagashi | traditional Japanese sweets |
| あんこ | anko | sweet red-bean paste |
| ロボット | robotto | robot |
| きらい / 嫌い | kirai | dislike, hate |
| にがて / 苦手 | nigate | not good with, scared of |
| ネズミ | nezumi | mouse, rat |
| みみ / 耳 | mimi | ear |
| おしいれ / 押入れ | oshiire | Japanese closet (for futons) |
| みらい / 未来 | mirai | future |

### Exercises

**Comprehension** — English answers are fine. (Better yet: pause before each answer and guess.)

1. Who is the boy with Doraemon?
2. Question 1: what are the three food choices, and which is Doraemon's favorite? What's inside it?
3. Question 2: what animal is Doraemon a robot of? Why doesn't he look like one?
4. Question 3: which animal is he scared of, and why? Why is that funny?
5. Question 4: where does he sleep? What do Japanese families normally put there?
6. Question 5: what century did he come from? Past or future?

**Shadowing** — out loud, twice each.

- ドラえもんのアニメを見たことがありますか？ (*Doraemon no anime o mita koto ga arimasu ka?*) — "Have you ever watched the Doraemon anime?"
- どら焼きは和菓子です。中にあんこが入っています。 (*Dorayaki wa wagashi desu. Naka ni anko ga haitte imasu.*) — "Dorayaki is a Japanese sweet. It has red-bean paste inside."
- ドラえもんは猫なのに、ネズミが苦手です。 (*Doraemon wa neko na noni, nezumi ga nigate desu.*) — "Even though Doraemon is a cat, he's scared of mice."
- 猫に見えませんよね。 (*Neko ni miemasen yo ne.*) — "He doesn't look like a cat, does he?"
- ドラえもんは未来から来ました。 (*Doraemon wa mirai kara kimashita.*) — "Doraemon came from the future."

**Speaking — make your own quiz.** Pick an anime character, or any character you like, and quiz an imaginary friend out loud.

1. このキャラクター、知ってますか？ ______ です。
2. ______ のアニメを見たことがありますか？ / 漫画を読んだことがありますか？
3. 第１問。 ______ の好きな食べ物は何でしょう？ １、______。２、______。３、______。
4. 正解は ______ です。
5. Then answer about yourself: 私は ______ が好きです。 ______ は嫌いです / 苦手です。
6. Bonus: ______ なのに、______ が苦手です。 (Even though I'm ~, I'm bad with ~.) Example: アメリカ人なのに、ハンバーガーが苦手です。

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 1</summary>

1. のび太くん (Nobita).
2. Cream puff (シュークリーム), apple pie (アップルパイ), dorayaki (どら焼き). The answer is #3, dorayaki. It's a traditional Japanese sweet (和菓子) with red-bean paste (あんこ) inside. He loves it (大好き).
3. A cat (猫型ロボット, "cat-shaped robot"). He has no ears (耳がない), so he doesn't look like a cat (猫に見えません). He had cat ears long ago, but not anymore.
4. Mice (ネズミ). The choices were rabbit (うさぎ), dog (犬), and mouse. Long ago a mouse chewed off his ears (ネズミに耳をかじられた), so it's a trauma (トラウマ): he runs away when he sees one (逃げます). It's funny because he's a cat that's scared of mice (猫なのに).
5. In the closet in Nobita's room (#2, 押入れの中). The other choices were inside Nobita's desk drawer (机の引き出し) and on the balcony (ベランダ). An 押入れ is like a closet where you put clothes and futons (服や布団).
6. The 22nd century (２２世紀): the future (未来). The other choices were the 20th century (the past, 過去) and the 23rd.

</details>

## Video 2: My daughter's favorite teacher (8:50)

[My Daughter's Favorite Teacher | Beginner Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=89A0ujOWRaE) — にほんごのじかん | Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)

Her daughter loved her nursery-school teacher. After graduating she kept missing her and crying before bed. Twice they went back to the nursery school to try to see her. Listen for how many months pass, and for the two different kinds of crying.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ほいくえん / 保育園 | hoikuen | nursery school, daycare |
| しょうがっこう / 小学校 | shōgakkō | elementary school |
| 〜ねんせい / 〜年生 | ~nensei | ~th grader (１年生 = first grader) |
| ことし / 今年 | kotoshi | this year |
| そつえん / 卒園 | sotsuen | graduating from nursery school |
| ともだち / 友達 | tomodachi | friend |
| なきます / 泣きます | nakimasu | cry |
| おわかれ / お別れ | owakare | saying goodbye |
| やさしい / 優しい | yasashii | kind, gentle |
| わかい / 若い | wakai | young |
| てがみ / 手紙 | tegami | letter |
| あいたい / 会いたい | aitai | want to see (someone), miss |
| たちました / 経ちました | tachimashita | (time) passed |
| はんとし / 半年 | hantoshi | half a year |
| うれしい / 嬉しい | ureshii | happy, glad |
| さびしい / 寂しい | sabishii | lonely, sad (missing someone) |

### Exercises

**Comprehension**

1. How old is her daughter, and what grade is she in?
2. How many years did the daughter go to nursery school? When did she graduate, and when did she start elementary school?
3. Why did she cry every night before graduation? What did she say?
4. What was her favorite teacher like? (Three words.)
5. What happened on the last day? Did she cry in front of the teacher?
6. What did the daughter want to do when she saw the teacher again?
7. The first visit, in July: did they see the teacher? Why not?
8. The second visit: how long after graduation was it, and what happened?
9. She cried in both parts of the story. What's the difference?

**Shadowing**

- 娘は保育園が大好きでした。 (*Musume wa hoikuen ga daisuki deshita.*) — "My daughter loved nursery school."
- 「先生とお別れしたくない」と言って泣いていました。 (*"Sensei to owakare shitakunai" to itte naite imashita.*) — "She cried, saying 'I don't want to say goodbye to my teacher.'"
- N先生は、若くて優しい先生でした。 (*Enu-sensei wa, wakakute yasashii sensei deshita.*) — "Ms. N was a young, kind teacher."
- 卒園して３ヶ月経ちました。 (*Sotsuen shite sankagetsu tachimashita.*) — "Three months passed after she graduated."
- 娘は嬉しくて泣いていました。 (*Musume wa ureshikute naite imashita.*) — "My daughter was crying because she was happy."

**Speaking — your favorite teacher.** She ends with a question for you: 子供の頃、好きな先生はいましたか？ Answer it out loud.

1. 子供の頃、好きな先生はいましたか？ → はい、いました。 / いいえ、いませんでした。
2. ______ 先生です。 (Use a name or just a letter, like she did.)
3. ______ くて、______ 先生でした。 Pick two: 優しい → 優しくて, 若い → 若くて, 面白い → 面白くて, 厳しい (strict) → 厳しくて.
4. 先生は「______」と言いました。 Something a teacher, coach, or parent used to say to you. English inside the quotes is fine for now.
5. Bonus: 今、______ に会いたいです。 Someone you miss.

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 2</summary>

1. Seven (７歳). First grade of elementary school (小学校１年生).
2. Five years, from age 1 to 6 (５年間). She graduated in March (３月で卒園) and started elementary school in April (４月に入学).
3. She loved her friends and teachers and didn't want to leave. She said 「保育園卒園したくないよ」「友達とお別れしたくない」「先生とお別れしたくない」.
4. Young, cute, and kind (若くて可愛くて優しい).
5. She gave the teacher a letter and a present, the teacher gave her a letter, and they took a photo together. She did **not** cry in front of the teacher; she held it in (我慢していた) and started crying once they got in the car.
6. Hug her (先生とぎゅーしたい).
7. No. Another teacher told them N-sensei had already gone home (もう帰りました). Too bad (残念でした).
8. Six months after graduation (半年). The teacher was there. They talked a little (学校楽しい？ お友達できた？), but the daughter was shy and only said うん. Then she got a big hug (ぎゅーっとしてもらいました).
9. Before, she cried because she was lonely and missed the teacher (寂しくて). At the end she cried because she was happy (嬉しくて). And mom was happy too (私も嬉しかったです).

## Read it

Today's reading is a **sweets-shop menu**. Bakeries and wagashi shops in Japan mix kanji, hiragana, and katakana on one board. Katakana is for Western sweets (シュークリーム, アップルパイ); kanji and hiragana are for Japanese ones (どら焼き, あんこ).

| Sign | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 和菓子 | わがし | Japanese sweets |
| 洋菓子 | ようがし | Western sweets (洋 = Western) |
| 〜堂 | 〜どう | common ending for shop names |
| 本日 | ほんじつ | today (sign word for 今日) |
| おすすめ | osusume | recommended |
| 売り切れ | うりきれ | sold out |
| つぶあん / こしあん | tsubuan / koshian | chunky / smooth bean paste |
| 〜個 / 〜個入り | 〜こ / 〜こいり | ~ pieces / box of ~ (Day 8) |
| 税込 | ぜいこみ | tax included (Day 5) |
| 秋限定 | あきげんてい | autumn only (Day 5) |

**The menu board**

```
【和菓子・洋菓子　さくら堂】
  ◆ 和菓子
    どら焼き（つぶあん）　　　１個　１８０円
    どら焼き（こしあん）　　　１個　１８０円
    抹茶どら焼き　　　　　　　１個　２２０円　【売り切れ】
  ◆ 洋菓子
    シュークリーム　　　　　　１個　２５０円
    アップルパイ　　　　　　　１個　３２０円
  ★ 本日のおすすめ：栗どら焼き（秋限定）　２６０円
  ◎ どら焼き５個入り　８５０円
  ※ 価格はすべて税込です
```

1. What's the name of the shop? What two kinds of sweets does it sell?
2. Read the Western sweets (洋菓子) section out loud. Where did you hear both of those words before?
3. You want the matcha dorayaki. Can you buy it?
4. What's today's recommendation? Can you get it in spring?
5. Doraemon wants five dorayaki. Is the box of five cheaper than buying five separately? By how much?
6. Do you have to pay extra tax?

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Read it</summary>

1. さくら堂 (Sakura-dō). Japanese sweets (和菓子) and Western sweets (洋菓子).
2. シュークリーム (cream puff) and アップルパイ (apple pie). Both were wrong answers in Question 1.
3. No, it's sold out (売り切れ).
4. Chestnut dorayaki (栗どら焼き), 260 yen. No: it's autumn only (秋限定).
5. Yes. Five at 180 yen = 900 yen; the box is 850 yen, so you save 50 yen.
6. No, all prices include tax (すべて税込).

_The menu is a practice example made for this lesson, not a real shop._

</details>

## Use it today

Say these out loud at least once today.

1. **〜たことがありますか？** (*~ta koto ga arimasu ka?*) — "Have you ever ~?" Make the past form (食べた, 見た, 行った, 読んだ) and add ことがありますか. Ask three: どら焼きを食べたことがありますか？ 日本に行ったことがありますか？ ドラえもんを見たことがありますか？ Then answer each: はい、あります。 / いいえ、ありません。
2. **〜が苦手です。** (*~ga nigate desu.*) — "I'm not good with ~ / ~ isn't my thing." Softer and more natural than 嫌い when you're talking to someone you just met. Say one food and one thing you're bad at: 納豆が苦手です。 朝が苦手です。
