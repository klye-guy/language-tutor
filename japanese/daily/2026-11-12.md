# Daily lesson — 2026-11-12

Day three is **a day out in the city** and **describing people**. Video 1 follows a couple into town to shop and pick a restaurant (it ends at a tempura place), so you'll hear lots of yesterday's 〜ました past tense in a real story. Videos 2 and 3 are anime-character guessing games: the teacher gives hints like "boy or girl," age, colors, and what the character is wearing, and you try to guess who it is before she tells you.

**Time:** about 20 minutes of video, plus exercises, a short reading piece, and two phrases to say out loud. It should fit in about 30 minutes.

**How to watch:** Play each video once all the way through without pausing to look things up. Follow the pictures. Japanese subtitles are fine; skip English if you can. For Videos 2 and 3, **pause right before the answer** (after she says 何のキャラクターでしょう？, "who's the character?") and say your guess out loud first.

All three are from the same teacher as yesterday's self-introduction and ramen videos, so her voice and pace will already feel familiar. Video 1 is the longest and the most useful for real life in Japan.

## Video 1: A day out, and tempura (9:53)

[A Day Out with My Husband: We Ate Tempura | Complete Beginner Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xWwcT414JdQ) — にほんごのじかん | Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)

Yuki and her husband go to a big shopping district in Fukuoka, each go to a different shop, then can't decide where to eat lunch. She walks you through everything on the table at a tempura restaurant, piece by piece.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| おっと / 夫 | otto | (my) husband |
| でかけました / 出かけました | dekakemashita | went out |
| まち / 町 | machi | town, part of a city |
| とかい / 都会 | tokai | big city |
| いなか / 田舎 | inaka | countryside |
| ふくや / 服屋 | fukuya | clothing store |
| ほんや / 本屋 | hon'ya | bookstore |
| なやみます / 悩みます | nayamimasu | I can't decide / I'm torn |
| ちゅうもんしました / 注文しました | chūmon shimashita | ordered |
| わりばし / 割り箸 | waribashi | disposable chopsticks |
| しゅるい / 種類 | shurui | kinds, types |

### Exercises

**Comprehension** — English answers are fine.

1. What's the name of the area they went to? Is it a big city or the countryside?
2. Where did her husband go, and what did he buy? Where did she go, and how many of what did she buy?
3. Name three restaurants they thought about before choosing. Which one did they pick, and why?
4. Before the tempura arrived, several things were put on the table. Name three.
5. How many kinds of tempura were there? Which one had she never eaten before? Which was her favorite?

**Shadowing** — say each line out loud twice.

- 夫と二人で天神に行きました。 (*Otto to futari de Tenjin ni ikimashita.*) — "My husband and I went to Tenjin, just the two of us."
- 私は本屋で本を買いました。 (*Watashi wa hon'ya de hon o kaimashita.*) — "I bought books at the bookstore."
- いろんなお店があって、悩みます。 (*Ironna omise ga atte, nayamimasu.*) — "There are all kinds of places, I can't decide."
- このお店にしました。 (*Kono omise ni shimashita.*) — "We went with this place." (〜にします means "I'll go with ~ / I'll have ~.")
- 全部おいしかったです。 (*Zenbu oishikatta desu.*) — "Everything was delicious."

**Speaking** — tell a short story about a real or imaginary day out, out loud. One sentence per line.

1. ______ と ______ に行きました。 (who you went with, and where)
2. ______ で ______ を買いました。 (which shop, and what you bought)
3. ______ を食べました。 (what you ate)
4. ______ がおいしかったです。 (what was good)

Example: 友達と東京に行きました。本屋で本を買いました。ラーメンを食べました。ラーメンがおいしかったです。

Bonus, for a restaurant in Japan: point at the menu and say **これにします。** (*Kore ni shimasu.*, "I'll have this.")

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 1</summary>

1. Tenjin (天神), a big city area (都会). Not the countryside (田舎じゃありません). It has lots of tall buildings.
2. Her husband went to a clothing store (服屋) and bought a new backpack (リュック). She went to a bookstore (本屋) and bought seven books (７冊).
3. Any three of: yakitori (焼き鳥), curry (カレー), sushi (寿司), pasta (パスタ), ramen (ラーメン). They picked a tempura restaurant (天ぷら屋) because they both like tempura (夫も私も天ぷらが好きです).
4. Any three of: water (水), tempura dipping sauce (天ぷらのつゆ), rice (ご飯), clear soup (お吸い物), disposable chopsticks (割り箸), and rice seasoning (ふりかけ).
5. Eight kinds (８種類): eggplant (茄子), pumpkin (かぼちゃ), shrimp (エビ), squid (イカ), chicken (鶏肉), burdock root (ごぼう), fish (魚), and tomato (トマト). Tomato tempura was the one she'd never had (初めて食べました). Chicken was her favorite (鶏肉が一番好きでした).

</details>

## Video 2: Guess the anime character #1 (4:39)

[Guess the Anime Character! #1 | Complete Beginner Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B3wDXOByG6I) — にほんごのじかん | Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)

Six hints about a famous anime character, each one acted out with pictures. Don't look up the answer; guess.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ヒント | hinto | hint |
| おとこのこ / 男の子 | otoko no ko | boy |
| おんなのこ / 女の子 | onna no ko | girl |
| メガネ | megane | glasses |
| かけています | kakete imasu | is wearing (glasses) |
| あたまがいい / 頭がいい | atama ga ii | smart |
| あかい / 赤い | akai | red |
| あおい / 青い | aoi | blue |
| たんてい / 探偵 | tantei | detective |
| あててください / 当ててください | atete kudasai | please guess |

### Exercises

**Comprehension**

1. How many hints does she give?
2. Boy or girl? How old?
3. What is he wearing on his face, and what's around his neck? What color is it, and what color is it *not*?
4. What's his job?
5. Pause before the answer. Who is it? Say your guess out loud: 「______ ですか？」

**Shadowing** — out loud, twice each.

- そのキャラクターは男の子です。 (*Sono kyarakutā wa otoko no ko desu.*)
- その男の子はメガネをかけています。 (*Sono otoko no ko wa megane o kakete imasu.*)
- 青い蝶ネクタイじゃありません。 (*Aoi chō-nekutai ja arimasen.*) — じゃありません is the everyday way to say ではありません from yesterday.
- 何のキャラクターでしょう？ (*Nan no kyarakutā deshō?*) — "Who's the character, do you think?"

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 2</summary>

1. Six hints (６つ).
2. A boy (男の子), seven years old (７歳).
3. Glasses (メガネをかけています) and a bow tie (蝶ネクタイ). It's red, not blue (赤い蝶ネクタイ、青い蝶ネクタイじゃありません). He's also very smart (頭がいい, 賢い).
4. A detective (探偵).
5. Conan (コナン), from *Detective Conan* (also called *Case Closed* in English).

</details>

Same game, a few more hints and more colors and clothing words. Again, pause and guess before the answer.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ねこ / 猫 | neko | cat |
| かっています / 飼っています | katte imasu | keeps (a pet) |
| くろい / 黒い | kuroi | black |
| しろい / 白い | shiroi | white |

| まじょ / 魔女 | majo | witch |

### Exercises

**Comprehension**

1. Boy or girl? How old?
2. What animal does she have, and what color is it?
3. What is she wearing, and what color is it?
4. What's on her head? Big or small? What color?
5. What is she holding, and what is she?
6. Pause before the answer and guess out loud. Bonus: what's the cat's name?

**Shadowing** — out loud, twice each.

- その女の子は黒い猫を飼っています。 (*Sono onna no ko wa kuroi neko o katte imasu.*)
- 青いワンピースを着ています。 (*Aoi wanpiisu o kite imasu.*)
- 大きくて赤いリボンをつけています。 (*Ōkikute akai ribon o tsukete imasu.*) — "She's wearing a big red ribbon."
- 皆さん、わかりましたか？ (*Minasan, wakarimashita ka?*) — "Did you all get it?"

**Speaking — make your own quiz.** Pick any anime character you like (or anyone famous) and give five hints out loud, like the teacher does. Use these frames:

1. そのキャラクターは 男の子 / 女の子 です。
2. ______ 歳です。 (Guess if you're not sure.)
3. ______ を着ています。 / メガネをかけています。
4. ______ い ______ です。 (a color + a thing: 赤いリボン, 黒い猫, 青い服…)
5. ______ じゃありません。 ______ です。 (a "not this, but that" hint)

Finish with: **何のキャラクターでしょう？** If you want, try it on Lang in chat and Lang will guess.

1. A girl (女の子), thirteen years old (１３歳).
2. A cat (猫). It's black, not white (黒い猫、白い猫じゃありません).
3. A blue dress (青いワンピース), not a red one.
4. A big red ribbon (大きくて赤いリボン). Not small, not yellow.

## Read it

Today's reading is the kind of thing you'd see in a Japanese shopping building: shop names ending in 屋 (*ya*, "shop"), a floor directory, and a tempura menu.

**Shop names** — the kanji 屋 (*ya*) on the end means "shop," like "-ery" in "bakery." Read each one out loud, then say what it sells.

1. 本屋 (ほんや)
2. 服屋 (ふくや)
3. 天ぷら屋 (てんぷらや)
4. ラーメン屋 (ラーメンや)
5. パン屋 (パンや)

**A floor directory** — 階 (*kai*) means "floor." 1階 is the ground floor, 2階 is one floor up, and so on.

```
5階  レストラン   天ぷら・すし・カレー
4階  本屋
3階  服屋
2階  カフェ
1階  コンビニ
```

6. You want tempura. Which floor do you go to?
7. You want to buy a book. Which floor?
8. You need a coffee. Which floor?

**A tempura menu** — sound out the kana. Which ones did you hear in Video 1?

```
えび天      300円
いか天      250円
かぼちゃ天  150円
なす天      150円
とり天      200円
```

9. Which is the most expensive? Which two are the cheapest?
10. Order the chicken out loud: 「______ にします。」

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Read it</summary>

1. *hon'ya*, bookstore (books)
2. *fukuya*, clothing store (clothes)
3. *tenpura-ya*, tempura restaurant
4. *rāmen-ya*, ramen shop
5. *pan-ya*, bakery (bread)
6. 5階 (*go-kai*, 5th floor), where the restaurants (レストラン) are.
7. 4階 (*yon-kai*).
8. 2階 (*ni-kai*), the カフェ (*kafe*, café).
9. えび天 (shrimp, 300円) costs the most. かぼちゃ天 (pumpkin) and なす天 (eggplant) are the cheapest, at 150円 each. 天 here is short for 天ぷら.
10. とり天にします。 (*Toriten ni shimasu.*)

</details>
