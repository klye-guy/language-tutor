# Daily lesson — 2026-11-15

Sunday is **nature and seasons day**: autumn in Japan. Mid-November is peak 紅葉 (*kōyō*, autumn leaves) season, one of the most popular times of year to visit. Video 1 is a short picture talk about Japanese autumn scenery: cosmos flowers, red dragonflies, golden rice fields, and leaves changing color. Video 2 is a slow walk through a seaside park on a cool autumn morning, with the teacher naming everything she sees.

**Time:** about 16 minutes of video, plus exercises, a reading piece on weather and opening-hours signs, and two phrases to say out loud.

**How to watch:** Play each video once all the way through without pausing to look things up. Both are labeled Complete Beginner, so today should feel easier than yesterday's snack video. Let the pictures carry you. Japanese subtitles are fine; skip English if you can.

Same teacher again. Listen for words you already know coming back: 山, 花, 木, 真ん中, 黄色/赤/茶色/緑, 〜ています, 〜じゃありません, and 歩いています.

## Video 1: Autumn scenery in Japan (6:19)

[Autumn Scenery in Japan | Complete Beginner Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdqGSdOsLXI) — にほんごのじかん | Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)

She talks through four photos of autumn in Japan, asking herself questions and answering them, just like a picture book.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| あき / 秋 | aki | autumn, fall (from yesterday) |
| しゃしん / 写真 | shashin | photo |
| ふうけい / 風景 | fūkei | scenery |
| さきます / 咲きます | sakimasu | (flowers) bloom |
| うすい / 薄い | usui | light, pale (color) |
| こい / 濃い | koi | dark, deep (color) |
| むし / 虫 | mushi | bug, insect |
| とんぼ | tonbo | dragonfly |
| とびます / 飛びます | tobimasu | fly |
| とまっています / 止まっています | tomatte imasu | is perched, has stopped |
| たんぼ / 田んぼ | tanbo | rice paddy |
| おこめ / お米 | o-kome | rice (uncooked) |
| こうよう / 紅葉 | kōyō | autumn leaves, leaves changing color |

### Exercises

**Comprehension** — English answers are fine.

1. What month and season is it?
2. Photo 1: what flower is it? She rules out two other flowers first. Which ones?
3. What colors are the flowers, and what color is the middle? What's behind them, and what's in the sky?
4. Photo 2: what bug is it? Is it flying? Where is it?
5. Photo 3: where is this? What does 稲 (*ine*, the rice plant) turn into, step by step?
6. What color does the rice turn in autumn?
7. Photo 4: name four colors of the trees. What is happening to the leaves?

**Shadowing** — say each line out loud twice.

- 今、10月です。秋です。 (*Ima, jūgatsu desu. Aki desu.*) — "It's October now. It's autumn."
- これはコスモスです。 (*Kore wa kosumosu desu.*) — "This is a cosmos."
- 何の花ですか？ 桜の花ですか？ 違います。 (*Nan no hana desu ka? Sakura no hana desu ka? Chigaimasu.*) — "What flower is it? A cherry blossom? Nope."
- 赤い花に赤いトンボが止まっています。 (*Akai hana ni akai tonbo ga tomatte imasu.*) — "A red dragonfly is sitting on a red flower."
- 日本の秋は紅葉がとっても綺麗です。 (*Nihon no aki wa kōyō ga tottemo kirei desu.*) — "Autumn leaves in Japan are really beautiful."

**Speaking — do your own picture talk.** Open any photo on your phone (a trip, your yard, your food). Describe it out loud the way she does: ask, guess wrong, then answer.

1. これは何ですか？ (*What is this?*)
2. ______ ですか？ 違います。 (guess something wrong first: 犬ですか？ 違います。)
3. これは ______ です。 (say what it really is)
4. ______ があります。 / ______ が見えます。 (what's in it: 山, 木, 車, 空…)
5. 色は ______ です。 (its color: 赤, 黄色, 緑, 茶色, 薄いピンク…)

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 1</summary>

1. October (10月), autumn (秋).
2. Cosmos (コスモス), an autumn flower. Not a cherry blossom (桜) and not a tulip (チューリップ).
3. White (白い) and pink: light pink (薄いピンク) and dark pink (濃いピンク). The middle is yellow (黄色). Mountains (山) behind them, and clouds (雲) in the sky.
4. A dragonfly (トンボ). There are lots in Japan in autumn. This one isn't flying (飛んでません); it's perched (止まっています) on a red flower. It's a red dragonfly (赤いトンボ). (She jokes: is it on my head? No.)
5. A rice paddy (田んぼ). 稲 → お米 (rice grain) → ご飯 (cooked rice, a meal).
6. Yellow, golden (黄色くなります).
7. Any four of: green (緑), yellow (黄色), orange (オレンジ), brown (茶色), red (赤). The leaves are changing color (紅葉しています), from green to red, yellow, and orange.

</details>

## Video 2: Autumn park vlog (9:50)

[Autumn Park Vlog | Complete Beginner Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XmbwWQ8A_TQ) — にほんごのじかん | Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)

A walk through a park by the sea on a weekday morning. She talks about the weather and temperature, picks up fallen leaves and pine cones, spots a crow, and finds a tiny shrine inside the park.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| こうえん / 公園 | kōen | park |
| うみ / 海 | umi | sea, ocean |
| てんき / 天気 | tenki | weather |
| くもり / 曇り | kumori | cloudy |
| きおん / 気温 | kion | temperature (weather) |
| すずしい / 涼しい | suzushii | cool (pleasant) |
| はっぱ / 葉っぱ | happa | leaf |
| おちば / 落ち葉 | ochiba | fallen leaves |
| まつぼっくり / 松ぼっくり | matsubokkuri | pine cone |
| からす | karasu | crow |
| ひろいました / 拾いました | hiroimashita | picked up |
| へいじつ / 平日 | heijitsu | weekday |
| しゅうまつ / 週末 | shūmatsu | weekend |
| じんじゃ / 神社 | jinja | (Shinto) shrine |
| ちゅうしゃじょう / 駐車場 | chūshajō | parking lot |

### Exercises

**Comprehension**

1. Where is she? What can she see from the park?
2. What's the weather like? What's the date and the temperature?
3. Is it hot or cold? What word does she use instead?
4. What's under the pine tree?
5. What bird does she see? What color is it, and what sound does it make?
6. She picks up fallen leaves. Name the four colors she sorts them into. Which one isn't a leaf?
7. Lots of people are jogging. Is she?
8. What day of the week is it? Is it the weekend? Why are there so few people? Who is there?
9. What does she find inside the park, and what marks it?
10. Why aren't the cherry trees blooming? When will they bloom?
11. Where is she at the end, and what is she about to do?

**Shadowing** — out loud, twice each.

- 今、私は公園にいます。 (*Ima, watashi wa kōen ni imasu.*) — "I'm at the park right now."
- 今日の天気は曇りです。 (*Kyō no tenki wa kumori desu.*) — "Today's weather is cloudy."
- 暑くないです。寒くないです。涼しいです。 (*Atsuku nai desu. Samuku nai desu. Suzushii desu.*) — "It's not hot. It's not cold. It's cool."
- 私は走ってません。歩いています。 (*Watashi wa hashittemasen. Aruite imasu.*) — "I'm not running. I'm walking."
- 春になったら咲きます。 (*Haru ni nattara sakimasu.*) — "They'll bloom when spring comes."

**Speaking — give today's weather report.** Look outside (or at your weather app) and say it out loud, in full sentences:

1. 今日は ______ 月 ______ 日です。 ______ 曜日です。 (date and day: 11月15日、日曜日)
2. 今日の天気は ______ です。 (晴れ *hare* sunny / 曇り / 雨 *ame* rain / 雪 *yuki* snow)
3. 気温は ______ 度です。 (Japan uses Celsius: 10°C = 50°F, 20°C = 68°F)
4. 今日は ______ です。 (暑い / 寒い / 涼しい / 暖かい *atatakai* warm) — then add what it's *not*: 寒くないです。
5. 今日は週末です。 ______ に行きたいです。 (somewhere you'd like to go today: 公園, カフェ, ラーメン屋…)

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 2</summary>

1. In a big park (広い公園) by the sea (海の近くの公園). She can see the sea (海が見えます).
2. Cloudy (曇り), lots of clouds. October 23rd (10月23日). 19°C (about 66°F).
3. Neither: it's cool (涼しいです). 暑くないです、寒くないです. Autumn is cool.
4. Pine cones (松ぼっくり), small ones. Fallen leaves are under the other trees.
5. A crow (カラス). It's black (黒い鳥) and goes カーカー (*kā kā*).
6. Yellow (黄色), orange (オレンジ), red (赤), and brown (茶色). The pine cone isn't a leaf (これは落ち葉じゃありません).
7. No. She isn't running (走ってません); she's walking (歩いています).
8. Friday (金曜日), a weekday (平日), not the weekend. It's a weekday morning, so the park is quiet (人が少ないです); no kids at the playground (アスレチック). A few grandpas and grandmas (おじいちゃん、おばあちゃん).
9. A small shrine (小さい神社), with a torii gate (鳥居). Also: a pine tree full of pine cones, yachts (ヨット), a barbecue area, and flowers in pink, white, yellow, purple, and orange.
10. It's autumn, not spring (今は秋だから咲いていません). They bloom when spring comes (春になったら咲きます).

## Read it

Today's reading is **weather and opening hours**: the weather app on a Japanese phone, and the hours sign on a shop, museum, or shrine. Two big kanji sets: weather words, and the days of the week, which show up on every hours sign and train timetable.

**Days of the week** — each is one kanji plus 曜日 (*yōbi*). Read them out loud.

| Kanji | Reading | Day | Literally |
|---|---|---|---|
| 月 | げつ | Monday | moon |
| 火 | か | Tuesday | fire |
| 水 | すい | Wednesday | water |
| 木 | もく | Thursday | tree (the same 木 from the videos) |
| 金 | きん | Friday | gold, money |
| 土 | ど | Saturday | earth, soil |
| 日 | にち | Sunday | sun |

**More words**

| Label | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 晴れ | はれ | sunny |
| 曇り | くもり | cloudy |
| 雨 | あめ | rain |
| 平日 | へいじつ | weekdays |
| 土日祝 | どにちしゅく | Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays |
| 休み / 定休日 | やすみ / ていきゅうび | closed / regular closing day |
| 〜時 / 〜分 | 〜じ / 〜ふん | ~ o'clock / ~ minutes |

**The weather app and the sign** — imagine you're planning a day out.

```
【天気予報】
  土   晴れ   18℃
  日   曇り   15℃
  月   雨     12℃

【もみじカフェ　営業時間】
  平日      10:00〜18:00
  土日祝     9:00〜20:00
  定休日     水曜日
```

1. Which day is sunny? Which day will it rain?
2. Which day is coldest? Is Saturday 暑い, 寒い, or 涼しい? (18°C is about 64°F.)
3. You want to visit the café on Sunday. What time does it open and close?
4. On Monday, what are the hours?
5. You arrive on a Wednesday. What happens?
6. Bonus: 営業 showed up on Day 2 (営業中). What does 営業時間 mean?

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Read it</summary>

1. Saturday (土) is sunny (晴れ). Monday (月) is rainy (雨).
2. Monday, 12°C. Saturday is cool (涼しい): not hot, not cold.
3. Sunday is a 土日祝 day: 9:00 to 20:00 (9 AM to 8 PM).
4. Monday is a weekday (平日): 10:00 to 18:00 (10 AM to 6 PM).
5. It's closed. 定休日 is 水曜日 (Wednesday).
6. Business hours, opening hours (営業 = doing business, 時間 = time).

_The forecast and café sign are a practice example made for this lesson, not a real place._

</details>

## Use it today

Say these out loud at least once today.

1. **今日は涼しいですね。** (*Kyō wa suzushii desu ne.*) — "It's cool today, isn't it?" The classic Japanese small-talk opener. Swap in whatever fits: 寒いですね, 暑いですね, いい天気ですね (*nice weather, huh*).
2. **〜じゃありません。〜です。** — "It's not ~. It's ~." Correct yourself like she does all week: これは落ち葉じゃありません。松ぼっくりです。 Try it on things around you: これはコーヒーじゃありません。お茶です。
