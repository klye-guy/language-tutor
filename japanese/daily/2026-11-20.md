# Daily lesson — 2026-11-20

Friday is **travel day**: getting to Japan. Last Friday was trains and stations; today we go one step earlier, to the **airport** (空港, *kūkō*). Video 1 is a picture talk that walks you through an airport step by step: getting there, checking in, dropping your bag, security, and waiting at the gate. Video 2 is the same teacher talking about every country she has visited and what she did in each one.

Two big conversation tools today:

- **〜たら / 〜てから** put actions in order: "after I check in, I drop my bag" (チェックインしたら、荷物を預けます). You met 〜たら on Day 4; today you hear it again and again, so it should click.
- **〜に行ったことがあります / ありません** ("I have / haven't been to ~"). Day 9 gave you the question 〜たことがありますか; Video 2 gives you dozens of answers. "Have you been to ~?" is one of the most common questions a Japanese person will ask you, and one of the easiest ways to keep the talk going.

**Time:** about 17 minutes of video, plus exercises, a reading piece on an airport departure board, and two phrases to say out loud.

**How to watch:** Play each video once all the way through without stopping. Both are picture talks with lots of repetition, so let the pictures carry you. If you have time, rewatch the first half of Video 1 and say the steps along with her.

Listen for words you already know coming back: 電車, バス, 駅, 地下鉄, 〜から〜まで, 〜くらい, 着きました, 〜たら, トイレ, 待ちます, 乗ります, 好き/大好き, 行きたいです, 〜たことがあります, 食べました, 見ました, 辛い, 海, 綺麗, 家族 words (母, 姉).

## Video 1: Airport (8:43)

[Airport | Complete Beginner Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Xk1utXkITM) — にほんごのじかん | Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)

She shows a photo and asks: is this a station? A bus stop? No, it's an airport. Then she goes from her house to the airport and through every step until she's sitting at the gate.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 空港 | くうこう / kūkō | airport |
| 飛行機 | ひこうき / hikōki | airplane |
| バス停 | バスてい / basu-tei | bus stop |
| 近い / 遠い | ちかい / とおい / chikai / tōi | close / far |
| 着きました / 到着しました | つきました / とうちゃくしました | arrived |
| 搭乗手続き | とうじょうてつづき / tōjō tetsuzuki | check-in (for a flight) — she calls it a hard word |
| チケット / パスポート | chiketto / pasupōto | ticket / passport |
| 必要です / 要ります | ひつようです / いります | is needed |
| 国内 / 海外 | こくない / かいがい / kokunai / kaigai | domestic / overseas |
| 荷物 | にもつ / nimotsu | luggage, bags |
| スーツケース | sūtsukēsu | suitcase |
| 預けます | あずけます / azukemasu | check (a bag), leave with someone |
| 保安検査 | ほあんけんさ / hoan kensa | security check |
| 通ります | とおります / tōrimasu | go through |
| 搭乗口 | とうじょうぐち / tōjō-guchi | boarding gate |

### Exercises

**Comprehension** — English answers are fine.

1. Before she says "airport," what two other places does she guess the photo might be?
2. What is an airport a place for (〜に乗る場所)?
3. How does she feel about planes and airports?
4. How does she get from her house to the airport, and how long does it take?
5. What's the first thing she does after arriving? What do you need for it?
6. When do you need a passport, and when don't you? Give her examples.
7. List the steps after check-in, in order, until she's sitting down.
8. What does she do right before going to the gate, and why?
9. Where does she want to fly to?

**Shadowing** — out loud, twice each. Keep her calm, slow rhythm.

- ここは空港です。空港は飛行機に乗る場所です。 (*Koko wa kūkō desu. Kūkō wa hikōki ni noru basho desu.*) — "This is an airport. An airport is a place where you board planes."
- 私の家から空港まで、地下鉄で４０分くらいです。 (*Watashi no ie kara kūkō made, chikatetsu de yonjuppun kurai desu.*) — "From my house to the airport is about 40 minutes by subway."
- 空港に着いたら、まず搭乗手続きをします。 (*Kūkō ni tsuitara, mazu tōjō tetsuzuki o shimasu.*) — "When I get to the airport, first I check in."
- チェックインしたら、次は荷物を預けます。 (*Chekku-in shitara, tsugi wa nimotsu o azukemasu.*) — "After checking in, next I drop off my bags."
- トイレに行ってから搭乗口に行きます。 (*Toire ni itte kara tōjō-guchi ni ikimasu.*) — "I go to the restroom, and then I go to the gate."
- 飛行機に乗ってどこに行きたいですか？ (*Hikōki ni notte doko ni ikitai desu ka?*) — "Where do you want to go by plane?"

**Speaking — your airport.** Answer out loud in full sentences.

1. 家の近くに空港はありますか？ → はい、あります。 / いいえ、ありません。
2. 家から空港まで、何で行きますか？何分くらいですか？ → 家から空港まで、______ で ______ くらいです。
3. 飛行機は好きですか？ → ______ 。 (大好きです / あまり好きじゃないです / 苦手です)

**Speaking — talk through the steps.** Describe your trip from your front door to the gate, using **〜たら** or **〜てから** to link each step. Aim for five sentences.

- 空港に着いたら、まず ______ 。
- ______ したら、次は ______ 。
- ______ てから、搭乗口に行きます。
- 搭乗口で ______ を待ちます。

Example chain: 家から空港までバスで行きます → 着いたら搭乗手続きをします → 荷物を預けます → 保安検査を受けます → コーヒーを買ってから搭乗口に行きます.

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 1</summary>

1. A train station (電車の駅) and a bus stop (バス停). It's neither.
2. A place to get on airplanes (飛行機に乗る場所).
3. She loves riding planes (飛行機に乗るのが大好き) and loves going to airports; the airport is one of her favorite places.
4. By subway (地下鉄), about 40 minutes (４０分くらい).
5. Check-in (搭乗手続き / チェックイン). You need a ticket (チケットが必要です).
6. Domestic flights inside Japan (Fukuoka to Tokyo, Osaka to Hokkaido): no passport needed. Overseas (Japan to the US, the UK, or Brazil): you need a passport.
7. Check in → drop off big bags like a suitcase (荷物を預けます) → security check (保安検査を受けます: go through a gate, they check your body and belongings) → restroom → boarding gate (搭乗口).
8. She goes to the restroom (トイレに行っておきます) because she wants to go before getting on the plane. Then she sits at the gate and waits for the plane (not a train!).
9. Disneyland (ディズニーランド).

</details>

## Video 2: Overseas Trip (8:36)

[Overseas Trip | Beginner Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OsyG7jLejjQ) — にほんごのじかん | Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)

The theme is 海外旅行 (overseas travel). She used to travel abroad a lot, and goes through every country she has been to, with a photo for each and one thing she did there.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 海外旅行 | かいがいりょこう / kaigai ryokō | overseas trip |
| 外国 | がいこく / gaikoku | foreign country |
| 以前 / 昔 | いぜん / むかし | before, in the past |
| 最近 | さいきん / saikin | lately |
| 人生で初めて | じんせいではじめて | for the first time in my life |
| おば / おばさん | oba / obasan | aunt |
| 国 | くに / kuni | country |
| アジア / ヨーロッパ | ajia / yōroppa | Asia / Europe |
| 北アメリカ / 南アメリカ | きた / みなみ アメリカ | North / South America |
| 登りました | のぼりました / noborimashita | climbed |
| 象 | ぞう / zō | elephant |
| 美術館 | びじゅつかん / bijutsukan | art museum |
| 森 / 湖 / 自然 | もり / みずうみ / しぜん | forest / lake / nature |
| 将来 / いつか | しょうらい / いつか | in the future / someday |

### Exercises

**Comprehension** — English answers are fine.

1. When was her very first trip abroad? Where did she go, and who went with her?
2. How many countries has she been to in total?
3. Which Asian countries has she visited? Which European ones?
4. Name three places in the world she has **never** been.
5. Match each country to what she did there: 中国, 韓国, タイ, ベトナム, インドネシア, イギリス, フランス, オーストラリア, ニュージーランド.
6. When was her last trip abroad? How many years before the video was that?
7. What does she want to do in the future?

**Shadowing** — out loud, twice each.

- 私は旅行が好きです。 (*Watashi wa ryokō ga suki desu.*) — "I like traveling."
- 人生で初めて外国に行きました。 (*Jinsei de hajimete gaikoku ni ikimashita.*) — "I went abroad for the first time in my life."
- 今までに９つの国に行きました。 (*Ima made ni kokonotsu no kuni ni ikimashita.*) — "Up to now I've been to nine countries."
- イギリスとフランスに行ったことがあります。 (*Igirisu to Furansu ni itta koto ga arimasu.*) — "I've been to England and France."
- 北アメリカには行ったことがありません。 (*Kita Amerika ni wa itta koto ga arimasen.*) — "I've never been to North America."
- いつかまた、海外旅行をたくさんしたいです。 (*Itsuka mata, kaigai ryokō o takusan shitai desu.*) — "Someday I want to travel abroad a lot again."

**Speaking — your travel map.** Talk about yourself out loud.

1. 海外旅行は好きですか？
2. 人生で初めての海外旅行はいつでしたか？どこに行きましたか？ → ______ 年に ______ に行きました。
3. 今までにいくつの国に行きましたか？ → ______ つの国に行きました。 (Counting: 一つ, 二つ, 三つ … 九つ, then 10か国, 11か国)
4. Name two places you **have** been and two you **haven't**: ______ に行ったことがあります。______ には行ったことがありません。
5. For one place you've been, say one thing you did there, like she does: ______ では ______ を食べました / 見ました / に行きました。

**Speaking — the question back.** In real conversation you don't just answer; you ask back. Practice the swap three times with different places:

- A: 日本に行ったことがありますか？
- B: いいえ、まだありません。でも、いつか行きたいです！ ______ さんは ______ に行ったことがありますか？

まだ (*mada*) = "not yet." いいえ、まだありません is a very natural answer when you plan to go someday.

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 2</summary>

1. 2002 (２００２年), to China (中国). Four people: her older sister (姉 / お姉ちゃん), her mother (母), her mother's older sister, her aunt (おば), and herself.
2. Nine countries (９つの国).
3. Asia: China, South Korea, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia. Europe: England (UK) and France.
4. Any three of: North America, South America, Africa, the Middle East (中東).
5. China: climbed the Great Wall (万里の長城に登りました). Korea: ate lots of spicy food (辛い料理をたくさん食べました). Thailand: rode an elephant (象に乗りました). Vietnam: wore an ao dai, a Vietnamese dress (アオザイを着ました). Indonesia: went to the beach; the sea is beautiful (海に行きました). England: saw Big Ben in London and ate fish and chips. France: went to the Louvre in Paris and saw the Mona Lisa. Australia: saw koalas and kangaroos. New Zealand: walked in nature (forests and lakes) (自然の中を歩きました).
6. 2013. That was 8 years earlier (the video was made in 2021), so she hasn't traveled abroad for 8 years (８年間海外旅行をしていません).
7. Travel abroad a lot again someday (いつかまた、将来、海外旅行をたくさんしたいです).

</details>

## Read it

Today's reading is an **airport departure board** (出発便, *shuppatsu-bin*). Big Japanese airports show English too, but smaller ones and the gate screens often show Japanese first, and delays and cancellations sometimes appear in kanji only.

| Sign | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 出発 / 到着 | しゅっぱつ / とうちゃく | departures / arrivals |
| 国内線 / 国際線 | こくないせん / こくさいせん | domestic flights / international flights |
| 便 / 便名 | びん / びんめい | flight / flight number |
| 行先 | いきさき | destination |
| 時刻 / 定刻 | じこく / ていこく | time / scheduled time, on time |
| 搭乗口 | とうじょうぐち | boarding gate (Video 1) |
| 搭乗中 | とうじょうちゅう | now boarding |
| 遅延 / 変更 | ちえん / へんこう | delayed / changed |
| 欠航 | けっこう | cancelled (flight) |
| 保安検査場 | ほあんけんさじょう | security checkpoint (Video 1) |
| 手荷物 | てにもつ | baggage, carry-on (荷物 from Video 1) |

The station words from Day 4 carry over: 〜番 numbers, 東/西, 乗り換え. So does 〜ゲート, which is often written with katakana instead of 搭乗口.

**The board**

```
【国内線　出発】                     現在時刻 9:05

  時刻    便名       行先         搭乗口   備考
  9:20   NH 241    福岡         12      搭乗中
  9:40   JL 313    札幌         5       定刻
  10:00  NH 18     大阪(伊丹)    8       遅延 10:45
  10:15  JL 507    沖縄(那覇)    21      欠航
  10:30  NH 465    鹿児島        12      搭乗口変更 → 15

  ※ 保安検査場は出発時刻の20分前までにお通りください
  ※ 手荷物のお預けは出発時刻の30分前までです
```

1. Is this board for domestic or international flights? Departures or arrivals?
2. Which flight is boarding right now? Where is it going, and from which gate?
3. You're on the flight to 札幌 (Sapporo). Is it running on time?
4. You're flying to 大阪 (Osaka). What time does it leave now?
5. Your friend is flying to 沖縄 (Okinawa). What's the bad news?
6. You're flying to 鹿児島 (Kagoshima). Where do you go?
7. For the 10:30 flight, what's the latest time you can check a bag? The latest time to get through security?
8. Do you need your passport for any of these flights? (Think back to Video 1.)

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Read it</summary>

1. Domestic (国内線), departures (出発).
2. NH 241 to Fukuoka (福岡), gate 12. Its status is 搭乗中 (now boarding).
3. Yes: 定刻 means on time.
4. 10:45. It was scheduled for 10:00 but it's delayed (遅延).
5. It's cancelled (欠航).
6. The gate changed (搭乗口変更) from 12 to 15, so go to gate 15.
7. Check bags 30 minutes before departure, so by 10:00. Get through security 20 minutes before, so by 10:10.
8. No. They're all domestic flights inside Japan, and like she said in Video 1, 国内の場合はパスポートは要りません. (A foreign visitor should still carry a passport in Japan, but the airline doesn't need it for the flight.)

_The departure board is a practice example made for this lesson, not real flight information._

</details>

## Use it today

Say these out loud at least once today.

1. **〜に行ったことがあります / 〜には行ったことがありません** (*~ni itta koto ga arimasu / ~ni wa itta koto ga arimasen*) — "I've been to ~ / I've never been to ~." Say three true sentences about places you've been and haven't been. Then ask someone the question: 〜に行ったことがありますか？ If you haven't been to Japan yet: **まだありません。でも、いつか行きたいです！**
2. **〜たら、〜ます / 〜てから、〜ます** (*~tara, ~masu / ~te kara, ~masu*) — "When/after ~, I'll ~." Narrate your evening in Japanese as you go: 家に帰ったら、晩ごはんを食べます。食べてから、日本語を勉強します。
