# Daily lesson — 2026-11-10

Welcome to day one. Today's videos are about a morning routine and breakfast, Japanese curry, and a short walk around Japan — beginner-level comprehensible input, spoken mostly in Japanese, with pictures or real footage to help you follow.

**Time:** about 17 minutes of video, plus exercises, a short reading piece, and two phrases to say out loud. The whole lesson should fit in about 30 minutes.

**How to watch:** Play each video once all the way through. Don't pause to look words up. Follow the pictures. Japanese subtitles are fine; skip English if you can. You will not catch every word, and that is expected. Listen for the key words below.

The curry video is labeled beginner (not super-beginner), so it may feel like a stretch. Stay with the pictures and the words you already looked at. That is enough for today.

## Video 1: Morning routine (4:57)

[Comprehensible Japanese - Morning Routine](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G-ArHStBCN8) — Nihongo-Learning

Yuta talks through his morning: breakfast, a walk, cleaning, then work.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| あさ / 朝 | asa | morning |
| あさごはん / 朝ごはん | asagohan | breakfast |
| コーヒー | kōhii | coffee |
| パン | pan | bread |
| たまご / 卵 | tamago | egg |
| たべます / 食べます | tabemasu | to eat |
| のみます / 飲みます | nomimasu | to drink |

### Exercises

**Comprehension** — English answers are fine.

1. What does Yuta drink in the morning?
2. What does he eat for breakfast?
3. What does he do after breakfast?
4. After he comes home, what does he do before work?

**Shadowing** — pause the video (or just read these) and say each line out loud twice.

- コーヒーを飲みます。 (*Kōhii o nomimasu.*)
- パンを食べます。 (*Pan o tabemasu.*)
- みなさんは朝ごはんに何を食べますか？ (*Minasan wa asagohan ni nani o tabemasu ka?*)

**Speaking** — say this out loud about your own breakfast. Fill in real food and drink.

- 朝ごはんに ______ を食べます。
- ______ を飲みます。

Example: 朝ごはんにパンを食べます。コーヒーを飲みます。

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 1</summary>

1. Coffee (コーヒー).
2. Bread and eggs (パン and 卵). He fries the egg, puts it on the bread, and eats it with coffee.
3. He goes for a walk (散歩, *sanpo*) for about 30 minutes.
4. He cleans the house, then he works.

</details>

## Video 2: Curry (8:56)

[👩🏻‍🏫🇯🇵Beginner Japanese Comprehensible Input - Curry🍛](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h8aHI9R4p0I) — Natural Japanese (NIJ)

A simple story about making a lot of curry and eating it for days. Pictures and repetition will carry you if the language moves a little fast.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| カレー | karē | curry |
| つくります / 作ります | tsukurimasu | to make |
| すき / 好き | suki | like |
| 好きじゃありません | suki ja arimasen | don't like |
| いただきます | itadakimasu | said before eating |
| おいしい | oishii | delicious |
| たべたい / 食べたい | tabetai | want to eat |

### Exercises

**Comprehension**

1. What is in her curry (the meat-and-vegetable one)?
2. Does her husband like curry?
3. Who ends up eating all the curry?
4. What does she say before she starts eating?

**Shadowing** — out loud, twice each.

- これは私のカレーです。 (*Kore wa watashi no karē desu.*)
- カレーが食べたい！ (*Karē ga tabetai!*)
- いただきます。
- 全部おいしかったです。 (*Zenbu oishikatta desu.*) — "It was all delicious."

**Speaking** — out loud, about you. Use real foods.

- 私は ______ が好きです。
- ______ は好きじゃありません。

Example: 私はカレーが好きです。コーヒーは好きじゃありません。

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 2</summary>

1. Meat, spinach, and onion (肉、ほうれん草、玉ねぎ).
2. No. He does not eat curry; he does not like it (好きじゃありません). He said 食べたくない ("I don't want to eat it").
3. She does, by herself (一人で). The cat cannot eat it, and the husband will not.
4. いただきます。

</details>

## Video 3: A walk in Japan (2:58)

[Easy Japanese - WALK IN JAPAN!! 【Beginner】](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SA9kWZaJnDs) — Nihongo-Learning

Short, slow, and visual: walking, a train, flowers, stairs.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| あるきます / 歩きます | arukimasu | to walk |
| やま / 山 | yama | mountain |
| でんしゃ / 電車 | densha | train |
| はな / 花 | hana | flower |
| かいだん / 階段 | kaidan | stairs |

### Exercises

**Comprehension**

1. What are they doing at the start of the video?
2. What vehicle do you see?
3. What color are the flowers?

**Shadowing** — out loud, twice each.

- 今、山を歩いています。 (*Ima, yama o aruite imasu.*) — "We're walking on the mountain now."
- 電車です。電車が走っています。 (*Densha desu. Densha ga hashitte imasu.*)
- これはピンクの花です。 (*Kore wa pinku no hana desu.*)

**Speaking** — say one of these out loud.

- 私は歩きます。
- 日本で歩きたいです。 (*Nihon de arukitai desu.*) — "I want to walk in Japan."

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 3</summary>

1. Walking (on a mountain / around the area). 歩いています。
2. A train (電車). It runs, then it stops.
3. Pink (ピンクの花).

</details>

## Read it

Read these out loud. They all connect to today's videos. Sound them out in kana first. The last items are two signs and a tiny menu — the kind of survival reading you will use in Japan.

**Kana from today's words**

コーヒー, パン, カレー, and メニュー are *katakana* (loanwords). You will see them on menus all the time. でんしゃ and あさごはん are *hiragana*.

1. コーヒー
2. パン
3. カレー
4. でんしゃ
5. あさごはん

**A tiny menu**

Imagine this in a shop window:

```
メニュー
カレー    800円
パン      200円
コーヒー  300円
```

6. Which food costs 800 yen?
7. How do you read メニュー and 円?

**Two survival signs** (stations, shops, buildings)

| Sign | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 入口 | いりぐち (*iriguchi*) | entrance |
| 出口 | でぐち (*deguchi*) | exit |

8. If you need to leave a station, which sign do you follow — 入口 or 出口?

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Read it</summary>

1. *kōhii* — coffee
2. *pan* — bread
3. *karē* — curry
4. *densha* — train
5. *asagohan* — breakfast
6. カレー (curry)
7. メニュー is *menyū* (menu). 円 is えん (*en*), yen.
8. 出口 (*deguchi*), exit.

</details>
