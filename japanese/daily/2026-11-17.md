# Daily lesson — 2026-11-17

Tuesday is **around town day**: finding things, buying things, and asking the way. Today's three videos all come from the same teacher you've been watching, and they build on each other. Video 1 is a short warm-up on *where* something is (on, under, in, left, right, between). Video 2 shows what she bought at the supermarket: how many are in each pack, when it expires, and how much everything cost. Video 3 is the big one: she walks around a town map and asks strangers for directions to a konbini, a park, city hall, a bank, and a hospital.

By the end you should be able to stop someone on the street, ask **この近くに〜はありますか？** or **〜にはどうやって行けばいいですか？**, and understand the answer.

**Time:** about 19 minutes of video, plus exercises, a reading piece on town signs and 右/左, and two phrases to say out loud.

**How to watch:** Play each video once all the way through without pausing to look things up. Videos 1 and 3 are labeled Complete Beginner; Video 2 is labeled Beginner but is short and very visual (she holds up every item). Japanese subtitles are fine; skip English if you can.

Listen for words you already know coming back: おにぎり, 紅茶, 卵, 〜枚, 遠い, バス停, 渡ります, 駅, 公園, 〜にいます, 〜があります, 〜てきました, 〜たいです, 違いますね.

## Video 1: Where is the apple? (2:51)

[Where is the Apple? | Complete Beginner Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UKSKP8GZ5tM) — にほんごのじかん | Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)

A red apple keeps moving around the drawing: on a table, on a box, under a tree, in a basket, next to a banana. Each time she asks りんごはどこにありますか？ and answers. Short and very easy, so treat it as a warm-up for the direction words in Video 3.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| りんご | ringo | apple |
| どこ | doko | where |
| 〜のうえ / 〜の上 | ~no ue | on top of ~ |
| 〜のした / 〜の下 | ~no shita | under ~ |
| 〜のなか / 〜の中 | ~no naka | inside ~ |
| 〜のみぎ / 〜の右 | ~no migi | to the right of ~ |
| 〜のひだり / 〜の左 | ~no hidari | to the left of ~ |
| AとBのあいだ / AとBの間 | A to B no aida | between A and B |
| はこ / 箱 | hako | box |
| き / 木 | ki | tree |
| いす / 椅子 | isu | chair |
| かご | kago | basket |
| ふくろ / 袋 | fukuro | bag |
| ぶどう | budō | grapes |

### Exercises

**Comprehension** — English answers are fine.

1. What color is the apple? Does she like apples?
2. List the places the apple goes, in order. (There are about a dozen; get as many as you can.)
3. Where is the apple at the very end, and why?

**Shadowing** — out loud, twice each.

- りんごはどこにありますか？ (*Ringo wa doko ni arimasu ka?*) — "Where's the apple?"
- 箱の上にあります。 (*Hako no ue ni arimasu.*) — "It's on the box."
- 椅子の下にあります。 (*Isu no shita ni arimasu.*) — "It's under the chair."
- バナナとぶどうの間にあります。 (*Banana to budō no aida ni arimasu.*) — "It's between the banana and the grapes."
- 私が食べました。美味しかった！ (*Watashi ga tabemashita. Oishikatta!*) — "I ate it. It was delicious!"

**Speaking — where's your stuff?** Pick five things around you (phone, keys, coffee cup, remote, a book) and say where each one is.

1. ______ はどこにありますか？ → ______ の上にあります。
2. Use each of 上, 下, 中, 右, 左 at least once.
3. Bonus: ______ と ______ の間にあります。
4. Bonus: hide something, then answer your own question: ______ はもうありません！ (*it's not there anymore!*)

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 1</summary>

1. Red (赤いりんご). Yes, she likes them (私は好きです).
2. On the table (テーブルの上, said as a question), on the box (箱の上), under the tree (木の下), under the chair (椅子の下), in the basket (カゴの中), in the bag (袋の中), to the right of the banana (バナナの右), to the right of the grapes (ぶどうの右), to the left of the banana (バナナの左), to the left of the grapes (ぶどうの左), between the banana and the grapes (バナナとぶどうの間).
3. It's gone (りんごはもうありません). She ate it (私が食べました), and it was delicious (美味しかった).

</details>

## Video 2: What I bought at the supermarket (5:33)

[What I Bought at a Supermarket | Beginner Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JKwdAszjMmk) — にほんごのじかん | Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)

She just got back from the supermarket and shows each item: what it is, how many are in the pack, who in the family eats it, the best-before date, and finally the price of each from the receipt.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| かいもの / 買い物 | kaimono | shopping |
| スーパー | sūpā | supermarket |
| ぎゅうにゅう / 牛乳 | gyūnyū | milk |
| しょくパン / 食パン | shokupan | sliced bread |
| ヨーグルト | yōguruto | yogurt |
| 〜こ / 〜個 | ~ko | counter for small objects |
| 〜いり / 〜入り | ~iri | pack of ~ (６枚入り = 6-slice pack) |
| いちごあじ / いちご味 | ichigo aji | strawberry flavor (味 from Day 5) |
| ときどき / 時々 | tokidoki | sometimes |
| しょうみきげん / 賞味期限 | shōmi kigen | best-before date |
| 〜がつ〜にち / 〜月〜日 | ~gatsu ~nichi | (month) (day): ５月８日 |
| レシート | reshiito | receipt |
| いくら | ikura | how much (price) |
| ごうけい / 合計 | gōkei | total |

### Exercises

**Comprehension**

1. How many things did she buy? Name them.
2. Who drinks the milk, and when? Does she?
3. How many slices are in the bread? When does the family eat it?
4. How many yogurts are in the pack, and what flavor? Who eats them?
5. How many eggs? What three dishes does she make with eggs?
6. She and her husband both like coffee. So when do they drink the tea?
7. Today is April 27. How many days do they have to finish the bread?
8. Price check: how much was the bread? The milk? The eggs? The total?

**Shadowing**

- 買い物に行ってきました。 (*Kaimono ni itte kimashita.*) — "I went shopping (and I'm back)."
- これは牛乳です。 (*Kore wa gyūnyū desu.*) — "This is milk."
- ６枚入りの食パンです。 (*Rokumai-iri no shokupan desu.*) — "It's a 6-slice pack of bread."
- 紅茶はいくらですか？ ２４９円です。 (*Kōcha wa ikura desu ka? Nihyaku yonjūkyū en desu.*) — "How much was the tea? 249 yen."
- 合計で９１８円でした。 (*Gōkei de kyūhyaku jūhachi en deshita.*) — "It came to 918 yen in total."

**Speaking — your last shopping trip.** Talk about the last time you went to a store (any store).

1. ______ に買い物に行ってきました。 (スーパー, コンビニ, or a store name)
2. これは ______ です。 Name three things you bought (or have in your kitchen right now): 牛乳, パン, 卵, コーヒー, りんご, バナナ…
3. ______ 個入り / ______ 枚入りです。 Say how many are in one pack.
4. ______ はいくらですか？ → ______ 円です。 Make up a price in yen and say it out loud. Try a three-digit number (３５０円, ９８０円).
5. 私は ______ を時々飲みます / 食べます。 Something you have only sometimes.

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 2</summary>

1. Five (全部で５つ): milk, bread, yogurt, eggs, and (black) tea.
2. Her kids drink it every morning (子どもたちが毎朝牛乳を飲みます). She doesn't drink milk.
3. Six slices (６枚入り). The whole family often eats it for breakfast (よく朝ごはんに食パンを食べます).
4. Four (４個セット), strawberry flavor (いちご味). She doesn't eat much of it; the other three in the family do.
5. Ten (１０個入り). Fried eggs (目玉焼き), boiled eggs (ゆで卵), and Japanese rolled omelet (卵焼き).
6. Sometimes (時々). They drink coffee every day, but tea now and then. The tea box has 25 bags.
7. Four days: the bread's best-before date is May 1 (２８、２９、３０、１ → あと４日).
8. Bread 160 yen, milk 168 yen, eggs 202 yen; total 918 yen (yogurt 139, tea 249).

</details>

## Video 3: Asking for directions (10:31)

[Asking for Directions | Complete Beginner Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lN317j67FS0) — にほんごのじかん | Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)

She puts a town map on screen, gets off the train, and needs to find five places. Each time she stops someone with すみません and asks the way, and you see the route drawn on the map. This is exactly what you'll do in Japan.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ちず / 地図 | chizu | map |
| まち / 街 | machi | town |
| たてもの / 建物 | tatemono | building |
| びょういん / 病院 | byōin | hospital |
| ぎんこう / 銀行 | ginkō | bank |
| しやくしょ / 市役所 | shiyakusho | city hall |
| タクシーのりば / タクシー乗り場 | takushii noriba | taxi stand (乗り場 from Day 4) |
| すみません | sumimasen | excuse me |
| このちかく / この近く | kono chikaku | near here |
| わかりません / 分かりません | wakarimasen | I don't know / don't understand |
| みち / 道 | michi | road, street |
| まっすぐいきます / まっすぐ行きます | massugu ikimasu | go straight |
| ひだりにまがります / 左に曲がります | hidari ni magarimasu | turn left |
| せんろ / 線路 | senro | railroad tracks |
| むかい / 向かい | mukai | across (the street) |
| めのまえ / 目の前 | me no mae | right in front of you |
| 〜くらいかかります | ~kurai kakarimasu | it takes about ~ |

### Exercises

**Comprehension**

1. Name four kinds of buildings she points out on the map.
2. Where is she at the start, and why does she want a konbini?
3. Where is the konbini?
4. What does she want to do at the park? How do you get to the park?
5. How do you get to city hall?
6. How do you get to the bank?
7. Why shouldn't she walk to the hospital? What does the person suggest, and where is that?
8. How does she get to her friend's house, and why?

**Shadowing**

- すみません。この近くにコンビニはありますか？ (*Sumimasen. Kono chikaku ni konbini wa arimasu ka?*) — "Excuse me, is there a convenience store near here?"
- 公園がどこにあるか分かりません。 (*Kōen ga doko ni aru ka wakarimasen.*) — "I don't know where the park is."
- この道をまっすぐ行って、左に曲がって… (*Kono michi o massugu itte, hidari ni magatte…*) — "Go straight along this street, turn left, and…"
- 市役所にはどうやって行けばいいですか？ (*Shiyakusho ni wa dō yatte ikeba ii desu ka?*) — "How do I get to city hall?"
- 歩くと３０分くらいかかります。タクシーで行った方がいいですよ。 (*Aruku to sanjuppun kurai kakarimasu. Takushii de itta hō ga ii desu yo.*) — "It takes about 30 minutes on foot. You'd better take a taxi."

**Speaking — role-play both sides.** Out loud, play the lost tourist, then the helpful local.

1. Tourist: すみません。この近くに ______ はありますか？ (駅, コンビニ, 銀行, トイレ, ラーメン屋)
2. Local: はい、すぐそこにありますよ。 or この道をまっすぐ行って、 ______ に曲がって、まっすぐ行ったところにありますよ。 (右 / 左)
3. Tourist: すみません。 ______ にはどうやって行けばいいですか？
4. Local: ちょっと遠いですよ。歩くと ______ 分くらいかかります。 ______ で行った方がいいですよ。 (タクシー, バス, 電車)
5. Tourist: あ、そうですか。ありがとうございます。
6. Bonus: give real directions from your front door to the nearest store or coffee shop, using まっすぐ, 右, 左, 曲がって.

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 3</summary>

1. Any four of: hospital (病院), restaurant (レストラン), convenience store (コンビニ), city hall (市役所), supermarket (スーパー), bank (銀行), department store (デパート). She also points out the station, bus stop, and taxi stand.
2. At the station; she just got off the train (駅で電車を降りたところ). She's hungry and wants a konbini rice ball (おにぎり).
3. Right outside the station, directly in front of it (駅を出たところ, 駅の目の前).
4. Sit on a park bench and eat her onigiri. Go straight along this street, turn left, and go straight again (まっすぐ行って、左に曲がって、まっすぐ行ったところ).
5. Go straight along this street and it's on your left (左手に見えます).
6. Go straight and you'll see the railroad tracks; the bank is just across the tracks (線路を渡ったところ).
7. It's a bit far: about 30 minutes on foot (歩くと３０分くらいかかります). Take a taxi (タクシーで行った方がいい); the taxi stand is right across the street (道の向かい).
8. By bus, because it's very far (遠いのでバスで行きます). The bus stop is right in front of her (目の前).

</details>

## Read it

Today's reading is **town and direction signs**. On a station exit map or a street signpost you'll see the same handful of kanji over and over. Four of them you learned by ear today: 上, 下, 右, 左.

| Sign | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 右 | みぎ | right |
| 左 | ひだり | left |
| 上 | うえ | up, above |
| 下 | した | down, below |
| 駅 | えき | station |
| 銀行 | ぎんこう | bank |
| 病院 | びょういん | hospital |
| 市役所 | しやくしょ | city hall |
| 交番 | こうばん | police box (where you can ask directions) |
| 薬局 | やっきょく | pharmacy |
| 現在地 | げんざいち | "you are here" (on maps) |
| 徒歩〜分 | とほ〜ふん/ぷん | ~ minutes on foot |

**The signpost** — you've just come out of the station's 東口 (east exit, from Day 4).

```
【案内】  ★ 現在地：駅 東口
  ← 左   銀行（徒歩２分）・ 交番（徒歩１分）
  → 右   病院（徒歩３０分）・ バス停（徒歩１分）
  ↑ まっすぐ  市役所（徒歩５分）・ 公園（徒歩１０分）
  ↓ 下   地下鉄（ちかてつ）
  ※ 薬局は銀行の向かいにあります
```

1. Where are you right now?
2. You need cash. Which way do you go, and how long is the walk?
3. You're lost and want to ask a police officer. Which way?
4. Is the hospital close? What would the person in Video 3 tell you to do?
5. Which way to city hall? To the park?
6. 地下鉄 is the subway. Is it up or down?
7. Where is the pharmacy?

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Read it</summary>

1. The east exit of the station (現在地：駅 東口).
2. Left (←), to the bank (銀行): a 2-minute walk (徒歩２分).
3. Left too: the police box (交番) is a 1-minute walk.
4. No, it's a 30-minute walk (徒歩３０分). Take a taxi (タクシーで行った方がいいですよ), or the bus: the bus stop is also to the right, 1 minute away.
5. Straight ahead (まっすぐ): city hall in 5 minutes, the park in 10.
6. Down (下). 地下 literally means "under the ground."
7. Across the street from the bank (銀行の向かい).

_The signpost is a practice example made for this lesson, not a real place._

</details>

## Use it today

Say these out loud at least once today.

1. **すみません。この近くに〜はありますか？** (*Sumimasen. Kono chikaku ni ~ wa arimasu ka?*) — "Excuse me, is there a ~ near here?" The single most useful sentence for a traveler. Practice it with three places: コンビニ, 駅, トイレ.
2. **〜はいくらですか？** (*~ wa ikura desu ka?*) — "How much is ~?" Point at something on your desk and price it: このコーヒーはいくらですか？ ３５０円です。
