# Daily lesson — 2026-11-16

Monday is **home and week day**: talking about your house, your weekdays vs. your weekend, and your hobbies. These are the topics that come up in almost every first conversation with a Japanese person: どこに住んでいますか？ 週末は何をしますか？ 趣味は何ですか？ After today you should be able to answer all three out loud.

Video 1 is a short tour of a Japanese house, room by room. Video 2 (the longest) compares a family's weekdays and weekends: waking up, cooking, eating out, crowded vs. empty places. It builds right on yesterday's 平日 and 週末. Video 3 is a new teacher (a man, so a different voice) talking about his four hobbies.

**Time:** about 19 minutes of video, plus exercises, a reading piece on restroom and building signs, and two phrases to say out loud.

**How to watch:** Play each video once all the way through without pausing to look things up. Videos 1 and 2 are labeled Complete Beginner. Video 3 is labeled Beginner, but it's short and repeats every sentence two or three times. Japanese subtitles are fine; skip English if you can.

Listen for words you already know coming back: 階段, 駐車場, 公園, 夫, 平日/週末, 月曜日〜日曜日, 一番好き, 〜の方が好き, 外食 (eating out), and 〜ています.

## Video 1: My house (4:12)

[My House | Complete Beginner Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JNgsHV9XErw) — にほんごのじかん | Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)

The same teacher from last week shows drawings of her house: the entrance, the first floor, the second floor, and outside. For each room she says what's in it and what the family does there.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| いえ / 家 | ie | house, home |
| しょうかいします / 紹介します | shōkai shimasu | introduce |
| 〜かい / 〜階 | ~kai | ~ floor (from Day 3) |
| げんかん / 玄関 | genkan | entryway (where shoes come off) |
| おふろ / お風呂 | o-furo | bath |
| トイレ | toire | toilet, restroom |
| だいどころ / 台所 | daidokoro | kitchen (also キッチン) |
| りょうり / 料理 | ryōri | cooking, food |
| テレビをみます / テレビを見ます | terebi o mimasu | watch TV |
| へや / 部屋 | heya | room |
| ねます / 寝ます | nemasu | sleep |
| しんしつ / 寝室 | shinshitsu | bedroom |
| つくえ / 机 | tsukue | desk |
| しごと / 仕事 | shigoto | work, job |
| あそびます / 遊びます | asobimasu | play |
| にわ / 庭 | niwa | yard, garden |

### Exercises

**Comprehension** — English answers are fine.

1. What shape is her house? How many floors?
2. How many people live there?
3. What's on the first floor? Name at least four things.
4. What does the family do at the table? On the sofa?
5. How many rooms are on the second floor? What's each one for?
6. Who works in the room with the big desk?
7. What's outside the house? What's missing?
8. So where do the kids go to play?

**Shadowing** — say each line out loud twice.

- 今日は、私の家を紹介します。 (*Kyō wa, watashi no ie o shōkai shimasu.*) — "Today I'll show you my house."
- ここは玄関です。 (*Koko wa genkan desu.*) — "This is the entryway."
- １階にはお風呂があります。トイレもあります。 (*Ikkai ni wa o-furo ga arimasu. Toire mo arimasu.*) — "There's a bath on the first floor. There's a toilet too."
- ここでテレビを見ます。 (*Koko de terebi o mimasu.*) — "We watch TV here."
- 私たちの家には、庭はありません。 (*Watashitachi no ie ni wa, niwa wa arimasen.*) — "Our house doesn't have a yard."

**Speaking — give a tour of where you are right now.** Walk around (or just look around) and narrate it out loud like she does. No need to describe anything private; any room works.

1. ここは ______ です。 (台所, 寝室, 玄関, トイレ, or 私の部屋 *my room*)
2. ______ があります。 ______ もあります。 (テレビ, 机, ソファー, テーブル, パソコン…)
3. ここで ______ ます。 (what you do here: 寝ます, 料理をします, 仕事をします, テレビを見ます, ご飯を食べます)
4. ______ はありません。 (something this room doesn't have)
5. ここは私が ______ 場所です。 (bonus: "this is the place where I ___," with the plain form: 寝る, 食べる, 仕事をする)

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 1</summary>

1. A square house (四角い家), two floors (２階建て).
2. Four: a family of four (家族４人で住んでいます).
3. Any four of: the bath (お風呂), toilet (トイレ), kitchen (台所, キッチン), a table (テーブル), and a sofa (ソファー). The stairs (階段) go up from there.
4. At the table they eat meals together (ご飯を食べます, みんなで食事をする場所). On the sofa they sit and watch TV (座ってテレビを見る場所).
5. Three rooms (３つ部屋). One has futons (布団): the bedroom (寝室), where everyone sleeps together. One has a big desk and a computer: a work room. One has lots of toys (おもちゃ): the kids' playroom.
6. Her husband (夫が仕事をします).
7. A parking spot for the car (駐車場, 車を停める場所). They don't have a yard (庭はありません).
8. To the nearby park (近くの公園), because there's no yard (庭がないので).

</details>

## Video 2: Weekday vs. weekend (9:55)

[Weekday VS Weekend | Complete Beginner Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=guQr6eg1BcA) — にほんごのじかん | Japanese Comprehensible Input (CI)

She goes through the days of the week, says which days she likes and why, then compares her family's weekdays and weekends: who works, who goes to school, who wakes up when, and when they cook or eat out.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| なんようび / 何曜日 | nan'yōbi | what day of the week |
| きのう / 昨日 | kinō | yesterday (from Day 5) |
| あした / 明日 | ashita | tomorrow |
| おととい / 一昨日 | ototoi | the day before yesterday |
| あさって / 明後日 | asatte | the day after tomorrow |
| がんばろう / 頑張ろう | ganbarō | let's do our best |
| やすみ / 休み | yasumi | day off, break |
| のんびり | nonbiri | relaxed, taking it easy |
| むすこ / 息子 | musuko | (my) son |
| むすめ / 娘 | musume | (my) daughter |
| がっこう / 学校 | gakkō | school |
| おきます / 起きます | okimasu | wake up, get up |
| はやく / 早く | hayaku | early, quickly |
| ちこく / 遅刻 | chikoku | being late |
| がいしょく / 外食 | gaishoku | eating out |
| こんでいます / 混んでいます | konde imasu | is crowded |
| すいています / 空いています | suite imasu | is empty, not crowded |

### Exercises

**Comprehension**

1. What day is it in the video? What was yesterday? What's tomorrow?
2. Which two days does she like, and why each one?
3. What's the word for Monday through Friday? For Saturday and Sunday?
4. On weekdays, what do she, her husband, her son, and her daughter each do?
5. What time does she wake up? Is it different on weekends?
6. What about her husband on weekdays vs. weekends?
7. The kids: when is it hard to get them up, and when do they get up on their own? What does she shout?
8. When does she cook every day? What does the family often do on weekends instead, and why?
9. Park and department store: which is more crowded, weekday or weekend?

**Shadowing** — out loud, twice each.

- 今日は何曜日ですか？ (*Kyō wa nan'yōbi desu ka?*) — "What day is it today?"
- 何曜日が一番好きですか？ (*Nan'yōbi ga ichiban suki desu ka?*) — "Which day do you like best?"
- 週末は私は仕事をしません。休みです。 (*Shūmatsu wa watashi wa shigoto o shimasen. Yasumi desu.*) — "I don't work on weekends. I'm off."
- 週末は遅くまで寝ています。 (*Shūmatsu wa osoku made nete imasu.*) — "On weekends (he) sleeps in late."
- 週末の方が人が多いです。 (*Shūmatsu no hō ga hito ga ōi desu.*) — "There are more people on the weekend."

**Speaking — your week, out loud.** Answer her questions about yourself in full sentences. (She asks the audience most of these directly in the video.)

1. 今日は何曜日ですか？ → 今日は ______ 曜日です。 昨日は ______ 曜日でした。
2. 何曜日が一番好きですか？ → ______ 曜日が一番好きです。 (add why with ので: 次の日が休みなので…)
3. 平日は何をしますか？ → 平日は ______ ます。 (仕事をします, 学校に行きます, 料理をします…)
4. 週末は何をしますか？ → 週末は ______ ます。 / 週末は ______ ません。 (仕事をしません, 早く起きません, 外食します…)
5. 何時に起きますか？ → 平日は ______ 時頃に起きます。週末は ______ 時頃に起きます。

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 2</summary>

1. Friday, May 13 (５月１３日金曜日). Yesterday was Thursday (木曜日でした); tomorrow is Saturday (土曜日). The day before yesterday was Wednesday, the day after tomorrow is Sunday.
2. Monday and Friday. Monday is the first day of the week, so she feels "OK, let's do our best this week!" (よし！頑張ろう！). Friday because the next day is a day off, so she can relax at night (夜のんびりできます).
3. 平日 (weekdays); 週末 (weekend), also 土日.
4. She works (making videos, 動画を作ります). Her husband works too. Her son goes to school (学校に行きます), Monday to Friday. Her daughter goes to daycare (保育園).
5. Around 6 (６時頃), early. Same on weekdays and weekends.
6. Weekdays he gets up around 7 because he has work. Weekends he doesn't get up early; he sleeps until around 10 (１０時頃まで寝ています).
7. Weekdays they won't get up, so she wakes them again and again: 起きて！起きて！早く起きて！遅刻するよ！ ("Get up! Hurry! You'll be late!"). On weekends they get up right away without being woken.
8. She cooks every weekday and they don't eat out. On weekends she wants to relax and doesn't want to cook (料理をしたくないです), so they often eat out (よく外食します).
9. Both are more crowded on the weekend (週末の方が人が多いです, 混んでいます). On weekdays they're empty (空いています, 人が少ないです).

</details>

## Video 3: What is your hobby? (4:53)

[Comprehensible Japanese 【Beginner】What is your hobby? みなさんの趣味は何ですか？](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jpoaxh-3uxc) — Nihongo-Learning

A new teacher, Yuta, explains what 趣味 means, then talks through his four hobbies using real objects (a book, a Kindle, headphones). Each sentence repeats two or three times.

### Before you watch

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| しゅみ / 趣味 | shumi | hobby |
| ほん / 本 | hon | book |
| よみます / 読みます | yomimasu | read |
| かみ / 紙 | kami | paper |
| おんがく / 音楽 | ongaku | music |
| ききます / 聞きます | kikimasu | listen, hear |
| ヘッドホン | heddohon | headphones |
| きょく / 曲 | kyoku | song, piece of music |
| みます / 見ます | mimasu | watch, see |
| えいご / 英語 | eigo | English |
| かんたん / 簡単 | kantan | easy, simple |
| 〜こと | ~koto | turns a verb into "doing ~" (本を読むこと = reading books) |

### Exercises

**Comprehension**

1. How does he explain what a hobby is?
2. Hobby 1: what kind of books does he read?
3. Paper books or Kindle: which does he like better now, and why?
4. Hobby 2: how does he listen to music? Who's his favorite band, and favorite song?
5. Hobby 3: what does he watch, and in what languages?
6. Hobby 4: what does he like to make, and why?

**Shadowing** — model sentences built from what he says (he says each one several times):

- 趣味は何ですか？ (*Shumi wa nan desu ka?*) — "What's your hobby?"
- 私の趣味は本を読むことです。 (*Watashi no shumi wa hon o yomu koto desu.*) — "My hobby is reading books."
- 紙の本も好きですが、キンドルの方が好きです。 (*Kami no hon mo suki desu ga, kindoru no hō ga suki desu.*) — "I like paper books too, but I like Kindle better."
- ヘッドホンで音楽を聞くのが好きです。 (*Heddohon de ongaku o kiku no ga suki desu.*) — "I like listening to music with headphones."
- 簡単で、おいしいからです。 (*Kantan de, oishii kara desu.*) — "Because it's easy and tasty."

**Speaking — your hobbies.** He ends by asking you: 趣味は何ですか？ Answer out loud, then keep going.

1. 私の趣味は ______ ことです。 (アニメを見る, 料理をする, 旅行をする *travel*, 本を読む, 日本語を勉強する *study Japanese*)
2. 一番好きな ______ は ______ です。 (my favorite anime / food / band is ___: 一番好きなアニメは〜です)
3. AとB、どっちが好きですか？ → ______ の方が好きです。 (from Day 5: 紙の本とキンドル, ラーメンとカレー, 平日と週末)
4. ______ からです。 (give a reason: 簡単だから, おいしいから, 面白い *omoshiroi* fun から)
5. Turn it around and ask an imaginary partner: ______ さんの趣味は何ですか？

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 3</summary>

1. Something you like; what you like to do on your days off, when you don't have work or school.
2. Business books.
3. He likes paper books too, but Kindle more now, because he can read it in the bath (お風呂).
4. With headphones (black ones). Oasis; his favorite song is "Whatever."
5. YouTube, in English or Spanish.
6. Spaghetti, because it's easy to make and delicious.

_His transcript was checked in English translation, so the shadowing lines are model sentences in his style rather than an exact word-for-word transcript._

</details>

## Read it

Today's reading is **restroom and building signs**, the ones you need in any station, mall, restaurant, or hotel in Japan. Several are single kanji you can spot from across a room.

| Sign | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| お手洗い | おてあらい | restroom (polite; literally "hand-washing") |
| トイレ | toire | toilet, restroom |
| 男 | おとこ | men (from 男の子, Day 3) |
| 女 | おんな | women (from 女の子, Day 3) |
| 押 / 押す | おす | push (on doors) |
| 引 / 引く | ひく | pull (on doors) |
| 使用中 | しようちゅう | occupied, in use (中 again, like 営業中) |
| 空き | あき | vacant (空 again, like 空いています) |
| 階段 | かいだん | stairs |
| エレベーター | erebētā | elevator |
| 非常口 | ひじょうぐち | emergency exit (口 = opening, like 入口/出口) |
| 禁煙 | きんえん | no smoking |

**The floor guide** — you're in a shopping building and need a restroom.

```
【フロアガイド】
  ３階   レストラン ・ 本屋 ・ お手洗い（男・女）
  ２階   服屋 ・ カフェ（禁煙）
  １階   コンビニ ・ 入口 ・ 出口
  ← 階段　　エレベーター →　　非常口 ↑

  （お手洗いのドア）　使用中
  （入口のドア）　　　押
```

1. Which floor has the restrooms? Are there both men's and women's?
2. You're at the café. Can you smoke there?
3. Which way to the stairs? To the elevator?
4. What does the arrow next to 非常口 point to?
5. The restroom door says 使用中. Can you go in? What would it say if you could?
6. The entrance door says 押. What do you do?
7. Bonus: you just came from the bookstore. What floor are you on?

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Read it</summary>

1. The 3rd floor (３階). Yes, both: 男 (men) and 女 (women).
2. No. 禁煙 means no smoking.
3. Stairs to the left (← 階段), elevator to the right (エレベーター →).
4. The emergency exit.
5. No, it's occupied (使用中). Vacant would say 空き.
6. Push it.
7. The 3rd floor (本屋 is on ３階).

_The floor guide is a practice example made for this lesson, not a real building._

</details>

## Use it today

Say these out loud at least once today.

1. **趣味は何ですか？** (*Shumi wa nan desu ka?*) — "What are your hobbies?" The easiest way to keep a conversation going with someone new. Have your own answer ready: 私の趣味は ______ ことです。
2. **週末は何をしましたか？** (*Shūmatsu wa nani o shimashita ka?*) — "What did you do this weekend?" The classic Monday question. Answer it about your own weekend: 週末は ______ ました。 (公園に行きました, 外食しました, 遅くまで寝ました…)
