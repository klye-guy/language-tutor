# Daily lesson — 2026-11-21

Saturday is **food day**. Last Saturday was lunch out and konbini snacks. Today we go to two things you will almost certainly eat in Japan: **noodles** (麺, *men*) and **yakitori** (焼き鳥, grilled chicken on skewers), the standard izakaya food. Both videos are from the same teacher. She talks over her own food photos, slowly and with lots of repetition.

Two big conversation tools today:

- **AよりBの方が〜 / Aが一番〜** compare things: "udon is thicker than soba" (蕎麦よりうどんの方が太いです), "I like tsukune best" (つくねが一番好きです). On Day 5 you learned Aの方が好き; today you add the よりも part and use it for more than "like."
- **〜という名前の〜 / 〜って言います** name things: "a spaghetti called Napolitan" (ナポリタンという名前のスパゲティ). She uses this every time she shows a new dish, and you can use it to teach a Japanese person about food from home.

**Time:** about 20 minutes of video, plus exercises, a reading piece on a yakitori and noodle menu, and two phrases to say out loud.

**How to watch:** Play each video once all the way through without stopping. Let the photos carry you. If you have time, rewatch the yakitori photos (about the first half of Video 2) and point at each skewer and name it before she does.

Listen for words you already know coming back: パン, ご飯, 好き/大好き/一番好き, AもBも, 〜じゃなくて, 天ぷら, ラーメン, 塩, 味噌, 卵, 〜の方が, 美味しかったです, 居酒屋, お酒, 晩ご飯, 〜たことがあります, 〜屋さん, 〜円, 高い/安い, 日本に来たら.

### Exercises

**Comprehension** — English answers are fine.

1. The first photo is bread. What are the second and third?
2. Which of the three does she like best? Which do her kids like best?
3. Name the five types of noodle dish she lists before the photos start.
4. Photo 1: hot or cold soba? What two things are on top?
5. Photo 2: how is udon different from soba? What's in her udon?
6. Photo 3: what flavor of ramen is it, and why did it smell so good?
7. Photo 4: what kind of ramen? What's on it? Does she like spicy food?
8. Photo 5: what is ナポリタン? Is it a dish from Naples? What's in it?

**Shadowing** — out loud, twice each.

- パンとご飯と麺、どれが一番好きですか？ (*Pan to gohan to men, dore ga ichiban suki desu ka?*) — "Bread, rice, and noodles: which do you like best?"
- 子供たちは、パンよりも麺の方が好きです。 (*Kodomo-tachi wa, pan yori mo men no hō ga suki desu.*) — "The kids like noodles more than bread."
- 冷たい蕎麦じゃなくて、温かい蕎麦です。 (*Tsumetai soba ja nakute, atatakai soba desu.*) — "It's not cold soba, it's hot soba."
- 蕎麦よりもうどんの方が太いです。 (*Soba yori mo udon no hō ga futoi desu.*) — "Udon is thicker than soba."
- 私は辛いものが好きです。 (*Watashi wa karai mono ga suki desu.*) — "I like spicy things."
- ナポリタンという名前のスパゲティです。 (*Naporitan to iu namae no supageti desu.*) — "It's a spaghetti called Napolitan."

**Speaking — your noodles.** Answer out loud in full sentences.

1. パンとご飯と麺、どれが一番好きですか？ → ______ が一番好きです。
2. Compare two of them: ______ よりも ______ の方が好きです。
3. 蕎麦とうどん、どっちが好きですか？ (Haven't tried one? → うどんは食べたことがありますが、蕎麦はまだありません。)
4. 辛いものは好きですか？ → はい、大好きです。 / 辛いものは苦手です。
5. 皆さんの国には、どんな麺料理がありますか？ → Pick one and describe it like she does: ______ という名前の麺料理です。______ と ______ が入っています。______ 味です。

**Speaking — compare anything.** Make three よりも〜の方が sentences with adjectives you know (大きい, 高い, 安い, 辛い, 太い, 好き):

- 東京より大阪の方が ______ 。
- ラーメンよりうどんの方が ______ 。
- コーヒーよりお茶の方が ______ 。

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Video 1</summary>

1. Rice (ご飯) and noodles (麺). The third is "neither bread nor rice" (パンでもご飯でもありません).
2. She likes all of them (全部好きです). Her two kids like noodles best: more than bread and more than rice.
3. Ramen, udon, yakisoba, spaghetti, soba (ラーメン、うどん、焼きそば、スパゲティ、蕎麦).
4. Hot soba in soup (冷たい蕎麦じゃなくて、温かい蕎麦). Beef (牛肉) and tempura: shrimp tempura and burdock tempura (ごぼうの天ぷら).
5. Udon is white and a little thick; soba is thin (蕎麦よりもうどんの方が太いです). Hers had egg (卵) and oysters (牡蠣).
6. Salt ramen (塩ラーメン, salt-flavored soup). Shrimp was mixed into the noodles themselves, so it smelled of shrimp (海老のいい香りがしました).
7. Spicy miso ramen (辛味噌ラーメン), with an egg and lots of vegetables. Yes, she likes spicy food, and it was spicy and delicious.
8. A ketchup-flavored spaghetti with sausage (ウィンナー), onion (玉ねぎ), and green pepper (ピーマン). It's named after Naples, but it's a Japanese dish, not an Italian one.

### Exercises

**Comprehension** — English answers are fine.

1. What is yakitori? Is it only chicken?
2. What is an izakaya (居酒屋)?
3. Where did her family get their yakitori, and where did they eat it?
4. Why does she think knowing yakitori names is useful?
5. 豚バラ: what meat, which part, and how was it seasoned?
6. The next three skewers all have タレ. What are they? Why is the thigh called かしわ on this menu?
7. Which skewer is her favorite? Which one does her daughter love?
8. What was the unusual skewer she ate for the first time?
9. Name two items she does **not** eat or has never eaten.
10. What did the 豚バラ, かしわ/鶏皮, and つくね cost per skewer?

**Shadowing** — out loud, twice each.

- これは焼き鳥と言います。 (*Kore wa yakitori to iimasu.*) — "This is called yakitori."
- 居酒屋はお酒を飲むお店です。 (*Izakaya wa o-sake o nomu o-mise desu.*) — "An izakaya is a place for drinking."
- 焼き鳥屋さんでテイクアウトして、家で食べました。 (*Yakitori-ya-san de teikuauto shite, ie de tabemashita.*) — "We got takeout from a yakitori shop and ate at home."
- 焼き鳥の中で、つくねが一番好きです。 (*Yakitori no naka de, tsukune ga ichiban suki desu.*) — "Of all yakitori, I like tsukune best."
- これは珍しいです。私は初めて食べました。 (*Kore wa mezurashii desu. Watashi wa hajimete tabemashita.*) — "This is unusual. I ate it for the first time."
- 豚バラは１本１８０円です。 (*Buta-bara wa ippon hyaku-hachijū en desu.*) — "Pork belly is 180 yen a skewer."

**Speaking — order at a yakitori counter.** Say it out loud as if to the staff. Use 〜本 (１本 *ippon*, ２本 *nihon*, ３本 *sanbon*) and Day 10's 〜でお願いします.

- すみません、つくねを２本ください。
- 鶏皮を１本と、豚バラを３本お願いします。
- 塩とタレ、どっちにしますか？ → タレでお願いします。 / 塩でお願いします。
- これは何ですか？ → (staff answer) → じゃあ、それを１本ください。

Now make your own order for two people: four different skewers, with counts and 塩 or タレ for each.

**Speaking — your opinion.** She asks these at the end. Answer them.

1. この値段はどう思いますか？高いですか？安いですか？ → 安いと思います。 / ちょっと高いと思います。
2. 焼き鳥を食べたことがありますか？ → はい、あります。______ が一番好きです。 / いいえ、まだありません。______ を食べてみたいです。
3. 珍しい料理を初めて食べたことがありますか？ → ______ を初めて食べました。美味しかったです。 / ちょっと苦手でした。

1. Meat and vegetables on skewers, grilled over a grill net (網の上で焼く). Mostly chicken (it's 焼き"鳥"), but there's also pork and vegetables.

## Read it

Today's reading is a **yakitori menu** like the ones on izakaya walls. Some are all kanji and kana with no pictures, so knowing a handful of words gets you a long way. Yakitori is usually priced **per skewer** (１本), and the menu tells you whether you can choose 塩 or タレ.

| Sign | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 焼き鳥 / 串焼き | やきとり / くしやき | yakitori / grilled skewers |
| 〜本 | ほん | per skewer (１本 = one skewer) |
| 塩 / タレ | しお / たれ | salt / glaze (Video 2) |
| 鶏 / 豚 / 牛 | とり / ぶた / ぎゅう | chicken / pork / beef |
| もも / 皮 | もも / かわ | thigh / skin (Video 2) |
| 盛り合わせ | もりあわせ | assortment, platter |
| 〜本盛り | 〜ほんもり | an assortment of ~ skewers |
| おまかせ | omakase | chef's choice |
| 野菜 | やさい | vegetables |
| お持ち帰り | おもちかえり | takeout (the sign word for テイクアウト) |
| 大盛り | おおもり | large portion (noodles, rice) |

Recycled: 円, 税込 (Day 5), 売り切れ (Day 9), 本日のおすすめ (Day 9), 〜名様 (Day 10).

**The menu**

```
【やきとり】　　１本の価格（税込）　※塩・タレお選びいただけます

  鶏もも ………………… 160円
  鶏皮 …………………… 160円
  つくね ………………… 200円
  豚バラ ………………… 180円　（塩のみ）
  手羽先 ………………… 220円
  ねぎま ………………… 180円
  砂肝 …………………… 150円　 売り切れ

【野菜】
  しいたけ ……………… 150円
  ししとう ……………… 130円

【盛り合わせ】
  おまかせ５本盛り …… 850円
  おまかせ８本盛り … 1,300円

【〆の麺】
  塩ラーメン …………… 650円
  温かいうどん ………… 500円　大盛り ＋100円

  ★本日のおすすめ：チーズつくね 250円
  ※お持ち帰りできます
```

1. Are these prices per skewer or per plate? Is tax included?
2. Can you choose salt or glaze? Which skewer doesn't give you a choice?
3. You want two chicken thigh, one chicken skin, and one tsukune. How much is that?
4. Which skewer can't you order tonight? Why?
5. What's today's special and how much is it?
6. You can't decide. What can you order instead, and what's the difference between the two options?
7. 〆 (*shime*) means the dish you finish the night with. What are the two 〆 options? How much is a large udon?
8. Can you take food home?
9. Bonus: ねぎま is chicken with ネギ (green onion) between the pieces. Which word from Video 2 is hiding in it?

<details>
<summary>Answer key — Read it</summary>

1. Per skewer (１本の価格), tax included (税込).
2. Yes (塩・タレお選びいただけます). 豚バラ is salt only (塩のみ), the same way it came in Video 2.
3. 160 × 2 + 160 + 200 = 680円.
4. 砂肝 (gizzard). It's sold out (売り切れ).
5. Cheese tsukune (チーズつくね), 250円.
6. The chef's-choice assortment (おまかせ盛り合わせ): 5 skewers for 850円 or 8 skewers for 1,300円.
7. Salt ramen (塩ラーメン, 650円) and hot udon (温かいうどん, 500円). Large udon is 500 + 100 = 600円.
8. Yes (お持ち帰りできます), just like her family's takeout.
9. ネギ, from the vegetable list (ネギ, 玉ねぎ…).

_The menu is a practice example made for this lesson, not a real shop's menu._

</details>

## Use it today

Say these out loud at least once today.

1. **AよりもBの方が〜です / 〜の中で、〜が一番〜です** (*A yori mo B no hō ga ~ desu / ~ no naka de, ~ ga ichiban ~ desu*) — "B is more ~ than A / of all ~, ~ is the most ~." At each meal today, compare it to something: 昨日の晩ご飯より今日の方が美味しいです。Then name your favorite: 麺料理の中で、ラーメンが一番好きです。
2. **〜を〜本ください / 〜でお願いします** (*~ o ~ hon kudasai / ~ de onegai shimasu*) — ordering by count. Order your meals out loud today in this style: 〜を１本ください, 〜を２つください, 塩でお願いします. 〜本 counts long, thin things: skewers, bottles, pens, bananas. Count some around you: ペンが３本あります。
